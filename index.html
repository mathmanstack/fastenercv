Connecting…

Keep the card and fasteners flat on the same light surface. Separate each piece from the others and the box edge. For manual calibration, click the four outer rectangle corners of the card (extend across its clipped corner). Recalibrate after moving the camera.

Measurements

Pointed tips suggest screws. Blunt threaded pieces can be bolts or machine screws; the image alone cannot reliably settle that distinction. M sizes are estimates of thread diameter, and thread pitch is not measured.
